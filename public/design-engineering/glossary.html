Glossary of Terms

Confused by the buzzwords? Don't worry, even we had to look these up! Here's your cheat sheet. 📖

Abstraction
Cohesion
Coupling
Pattern
Refactoring

Abstraction

The process of conceptualizing a system by focusing on the most important aspects while ignoring distracting implementation details.

Architecture

The overall structure of the software system, defining the fundamental subsystems, relationships, and deployment patterns.

Cohesion

A qualitative indication of how strongly related the internal responsibilities of a single module are. High cohesion is desirable.

Coupling

A qualitative indication of the degree to which a module relies on other modules. Low coupling is desirable.

Design Pattern

A proven, documented solution to a recurring design problem in a specific context.

Refactoring

A reorganization technique that simplifies the design (or code) of a component without changing its external behavior or semantics.

Separation of Concerns

A design principle stating that a complex problem can be handled more easily if it is subdivided into pieces that can each be solved and/or updated independently.
