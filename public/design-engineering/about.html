Instructor Guide & About

How to Teach with this Site

This site is effectively a textbook, a presentation tool, and an active learning workbook built into one offline-first package.

  1. Hook up to a projector and use the Presenter Toggle (found on the homepage or via console) to increase font sizes and hide sidebars.
  2. Turn on Instructor Notes to display yellow callouts that give you real-world anecdotes to share during class.
  3. Navigate using the Next/Prev buttons at the bottom of the screen.

Suggested 3-Hour Pacing Plan

Hour Content Focus Activity
Hour 1 Lecture 10: Intro, Cohesion/Coupling, Model Elements Group Discussion: Define the 5 elements for a smart thermostat.
Hour 2 Lecture 11: Architecture & Data Interactive Tool: "Case Study Generator". Students justify architectures.
Hour 3 Lecture 12: Deep Dives & Quality Self-testing: Flashcards and Quizzes on the Tools page.

Common Student Misconceptions (Be Prepared to Re-explain)

  • Misconception: Architecture is the same as design.
    Correction: Architecture is a HIGH-LEVEL subset of the overall Design Model.
  • Misconception: UI is the only interface.
    Correction: System-to-system APIs are heavily evaluated under Interface Design.
