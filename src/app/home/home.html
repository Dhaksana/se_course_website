23CSE311 – Software Engineering

Department of Computer Science & Engineering, ASC

About the Course

This course introduces essential concepts of software engineering including SDLC models, requirements engineering, design principles, testing strategies, and project management. Students will learn how to analyze, design, implement, test, and maintain high-quality software systems.
