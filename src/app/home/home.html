23CSE311 – Software Engineering

Department of Computer Science & Engineering, ASC

About the Course

This course introduces the fundamentals of Software Engineering through real-world industry examples. Students learn Agile development practices and gain hands-on experience with modern tools. The course prepares learners to be industry-ready with practical skills used in Agile and DevOps environments. Students will learn how to analyze, design, implement, test, and maintain high-quality software systems.
