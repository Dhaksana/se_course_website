Design Engineering

Master the critical bridge between what the client thinks they want and what we actually build. Learn how to architect, structure, and refine software systems.

Overview Lectures Tools & Quizzes

Learning Outcomes

What You Will Understand

  • • The purpose of design in the software engineering process
  • • Core design concepts: abstraction, modularity, and refinement
  • • The five elements of the Design Model
  • • How to evaluate design quality and architectural styles

What You Will Be Able To Do 🛠️

  • • Map requirements to design structures
  • • Apply component-level and interface design principles
  • • Identify and mitigate common design mistakes
  • • Answer exam-level questions on software design trade-offs

Course Structure

Lecture 10

Introduction to Design Engineering, Core Design Concepts, and the overall Design Model.

Lecture 11

Translating information models into data structures and defining the structural architecture.

Lecture 12

Interfaces, Components, Deployment mapping, Patterns, and evaluating for Quality.
