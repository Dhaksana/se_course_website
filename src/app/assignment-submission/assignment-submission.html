Assignment Submission Guidelines

Applicable to Section D Only.

Submission Schedule

All assignments are due the proceeding Tuesday at 9:30PM after an assignment has been given.

Late Submission Penalties

Late submissions attract the following penalties in case of a graded assignment:

Type Time Period Reduction
Penalty 1 Up to 48 hours late 20% of the score
Penalty 2 Up to 96 hours late 40% of the score
Penalty 3 Over 96 hours late 100% of the score
